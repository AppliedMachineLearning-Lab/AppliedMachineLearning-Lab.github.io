Last week, **Deutschlandfunk Kultur's** pop culture show **Kompressor** interviewed me about **Jev**, the new "System 1" model from the San Francisco start-up Typesafe AI.

Want to hear how a lightning-fast AI model could moderate thousands of Twitch chat messages per minute, play Doom, or flag emergencies at a festival in a split second? In the radio interview, I explain what sets Jev apart from LLMs like ChatGPT or Claude: rather than writing essays, it ticks boxes in a multiple-choice test, making fast, reflexive decisions instead of slow, deliberate ones. We also discuss whether Jev is the next big hype, and why I think its future lies alongside LLMs rather than in competition with them.

You can listen to the full interview (in German), **"Blitzschnelle KI – Was bringt JEV der Popkultur?"**, on the [Deutschlandfunk Kultur website](https://www.deutschlandfunkkultur.de/blitzschnelle-ki-was-bringt-jev-der-popkultur-100.html).

We have also put Jev to the test ourselves: in our paper [**"Evaluating and Benchmarking the System One Model Jev"**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37647), we benchmark it zero-shot on 37 datasets covering classification, routing, moderation, and rubric scoring, as well as on sequential decision-making tasks.
